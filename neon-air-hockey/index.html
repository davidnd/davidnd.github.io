Privacy policy

What the game stores

Your settings, your chosen difficulty and a count of matches played are stored on your device. The game has no accounts and no chat, and it does not send any of this to the developer. Deleting the game removes it.

Ads

The game is free and can show ads between matches. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, prevent fraud and fix problems, Google's software in the game can collect:

  • device identifiers, including Apple's advertising identifier if you allow tracking
  • approximate location, worked out from your internet address
  • ads you were shown and how you interacted with them and the game
  • crash, performance and other diagnostic data

The game does not ask for your precise location. Google explains how it uses this data in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy. Google may share data with its advertising partners as described there.

Your choices

iOS asks whether the game may track you. The game plays the same whichever you choose, and you can change your answer in Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. If you decline, you may still see ads, but Apple's advertising identifier is not available to them.

Where the law requires it, the game asks for consent before requesting ads. You can change that answer at any time from Ad Choices on the main menu, which appears where this applies.

Children

The game is made for a general audience and is not directed at children. Ads are limited to those Google rates as suitable for general audiences. If you believe a child's data has been collected, email the address below.
