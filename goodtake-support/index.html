Support · Privacy policy

Support

For help with goodtake, email goodtake.app@gmail.com. Include your goodtake and iOS versions and describe what happened.

To restore Premium, open the Premium screen in goodtake and tap Restore purchases. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Privacy policy

Photo library

goodtake requests photo library access to find similar photos, suggest shots to keep, and help you review photos and videos. Analysis runs on your device. goodtake does not upload your photos, videos, or analysis results. For originals stored in iCloud Photos, goodtake can ask Apple's Photos service to download them. The first scan downloads from iCloud over Wi-Fi only. After that, new photos, and photos and videos you open, may be downloaded over mobile data.

Local data

goodtake stores analysis results, review progress, and preferences locally. You can change or withdraw photo access in iOS Settings. Deleting goodtake removes its local app data and leaves your Photos library intact. When you confirm a photo deletion, Apple manages Recently Deleted and iCloud Photos syncing.

Subscriptions

Apple processes purchases and billing through the App Store. goodtake uses StoreKit transaction information to check and restore Premium access. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings. Apple's privacy policy applies to its services.

Tracking and sharing

goodtake does not use advertising trackers or send photo library data, review history, or app preferences to the developer or third parties.

Contact

For privacy questions, email goodtake.app@gmail.com. If this policy changes, the updated version is published in the app and on goodtake's support page.
