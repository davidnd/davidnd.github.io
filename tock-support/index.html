Support · Privacy policy

Support

For help with Tock, email davidnd.dev@gmail.com. Include your Tock and macOS versions and describe what happened. Please omit private reminder text unless needed to explain the issue.

Tock lives in the menu bar. Click its icon to open your reminders, or press Control–Command–R to add one. You can change shortcuts in Settings.

To restore a purchase, open Settings or Trial & Purchase in the menu and choose Restore Purchases.

Privacy policy

Tock stores your reminders, timers, settings and sync state locally on your Mac. Tock does not send this information to the developer. It does not include advertising, tracking or analytics, and does not require a Tock account.

Optional Apple Reminders sync

If you enable sync, Tock requests access to Apple Reminders and synchronizes reminders with a list named Tock. It reads and writes that list to keep reminders in step. Timers remain on your Mac. Apple Reminders may synchronize the list through your configured account, such as iCloud. That service is governed by its provider's privacy policy. You can turn sync off in Tock or revoke access in System Settings.

Notifications and shortcuts

If you choose macOS notifications, Tock requests permission to display reminder notifications. Global shortcuts let you open Tock without switching apps. Tock does not record your typing in other apps.

Your choices

You can edit or delete reminders in Tock. Turning sync off does not delete reminders already copied to Apple Reminders. Remove those separately in Apple Reminders if desired. Local app data remains until you delete it; uninstalling the application alone may leave that data behind.

External links and purchases

Links open in your browser. The destination website has its own privacy policy. App Store purchases and billing are handled by Apple under Apple's policies; Tock does not receive your payment card details.
